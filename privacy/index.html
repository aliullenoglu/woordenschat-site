Privacy policy

Woordenschat: Dutch Vocabulary · Last updated 6 November 2026

Woordenschat is made by Ali Ullenoglu. The app does not collect any data about you.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app contains no third-party code.
  • Your progress stays with you. It is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, it is also stored in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. I cannot see it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I receive no payment or personal details.
  • Speech uses the voices installed on your device. Nothing you type or hear is sent anywhere.
  • Backups you export are files you control.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on, are local notifications scheduled on your device.

If you email me, I use your email address only to reply, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Questions: aliullenoglu@gmail.com.
