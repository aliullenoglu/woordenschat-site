Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Settings → Purchases and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases also work for family members through Family Sharing.

How does sync work?

Your progress syncs through your own iCloud account. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on every device, and that iCloud Drive is on. Sync can take a minute.

Which themes are free?

The first three A1 themes. After that you can buy a level once (A1, A2, B1, B2 or C1) or all levels together. There is no subscription.

I don't hear any audio.

The app uses the Dutch voices installed on your device. Check that the ringer is on, or download a Dutch voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices (iPhone and iPad) or System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content (Mac).

Can I switch the interface to English?

Yes. The interface is in simple Dutch by default, for immersion. Change it in the app's Settings.

I found a mistake in a word or sentence.

Please email it, with the theme name and the word. Corrections go into the next update.
